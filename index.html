2. What MonitorVC does

MVC is an Android application designed to estimate how many short-form videos you view in Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. It can show a count panel, compare activity with goals you configure, maintain a local history, create weekly and monthly reports, and optionally display an on-screen reminder when a configured limit is reached.

The App is an informational habit-awareness tool. It is not a medical, psychological or diagnostic service, does not promise to change anyone's habits, and should not be relied on as the sole basis for health, safety, educational, employment or other high-impact decisions.

3. Supported apps and device compatibility

In version 3.2, video counting is intended for the standard versions of Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and the global version of TikTok. It does not promise support for Instagram Lite, TikTok Lite, regional TikTok variants, other video platforms, or future or modified versions of these apps. The App requires a compatible Android device (Android 9 or later is the intended minimum for this release).

4. Video counts are estimates, not guaranteed measurements

MVC does not have access to an official "video viewed" event from the supported apps. It estimates video changes from Android accessibility and scrolling events. It therefore cannot guarantee that every video will be counted, or that every count will be correct.

Depending on the third-party app, its version, the device and how you interact with it, the counter may:

  • miss videos that you viewed;
  • occasionally count a video more than once;
  • fail to count some upward scrolls or returns to repeated items in a feed;
  • occasionally count an item that is not a short video because of how a supported app exposes its interface to Android accessibility services; or
  • become less accurate or stop working after an update to Android, a supported app or a device interface.

Under ordinary scrolling, MVC is designed to provide a useful approximation for many videos, but the daily total, time estimates, session counts, platform breakdowns and derived statistics are not certified, exact or suitable as an audit record. Do not assume that the number is complete or precise.

If you disable the service, Android restricts it, the service stops, or the supported app does not expose the expected events, counting may stop or become incomplete.

MVC recognizes a limited list of Android home-screen launchers. It may not recognize niche, customized or newly released launchers. This can affect the App's ability to detect when you return to the home screen or MVC, save or finalize a day's activity, and handle the change of date as intended. The panel may occasionally remain partially visible or appear stuck at a particular opacity instead of fading away as intended. Its behavior may also be unusual if a new video is counted during a fade animation. Interacting directly with the phone, the MVC app or the home-screen launcher may allow the interface to recover, but this is not guaranteed.

Display size, screen resolution, font scaling, manufacturer customizations and Android version may affect the placement or appearance of interface elements. Some devices may show extra line breaks, clipped text or other layout differences even though the interface has been designed with these differences in mind.

Reports are estimates for personal reference, not official, certified or independently verified measurements.
